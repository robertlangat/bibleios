# Bible Daily — Support

Thank you for using Bible Daily. Here are answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

## Contact us

- **Report a problem or ask a question:** [open a support request](https://github.com/robertlangat/bibleios/issues/new). A free GitHub account is needed.
- **Email:** use the developer contact shown on Bible Daily's App Store page.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Bible Daily need the internet?**
No. The complete Bible is stored on your iPhone, so reading, search, and your bookmarks, highlights and notes all work offline. The internet is only used to show ads.

**Which Bible translation is included?**
The World English Bible (WEB), a modern English translation of all 66 books. It is in the Public Domain.

**How do I find a verse quickly?**
Open the **Search** tab and type a reference such as `John 3:16`, `Jn 3:16` or `Psalm 23`, or a word or phrase such as `love one another`. Tap a result to open it.

**How do I bookmark, highlight or add a note?**
While reading, tap any verse. Choose **Bookmark**, **Note**, **Copy**, **Share**, or a highlight colour. Everything you save appears in the **Saved** tab.

**Where are my notes stored? Will I lose them?**
Your notes, bookmarks and highlights are stored only on your iPhone, and there is no account. They are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer backup). Deleting the app deletes them.

**How do I change the text size or use dark mode?**
Use **A−** and **A+** at the top of the reader, or open **Home › Settings** (gear icon) for the text size slider and Light, Dark or Phone theme.

**The daily reminder doesn't arrive.**
Open **Home › Settings**, turn on **Remind me every day**, and allow notifications when asked. If you said no earlier, turn notifications on in the iPhone **Settings › Notifications › Bible Daily**. Use **Send a test reminder now** to check.

**Why do I see ads? Can I control ad tracking?**
Ads keep Bible Daily free. You choose whether to allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this any time in the iPhone **Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking**. Where required, **Settings › Ad privacy choices** in the app lets you review your consent.
